# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Job seekers in Argentina who are evaluating companies, preparing applications, or preparing for interviews. They may be researching companies from any industry, often with limited time and incomplete public information.

## Product Purpose

Radar Laboral helps users investigate a company and turn reliable public evidence into a Spanish-language preparation report. Success means a user can understand what the company does, what is known or uncertain, how to prepare for interviews, and how to adapt their CV safely without inventing facts.

## Brand Personality

Practical, trustworthy, precise.

The product should feel like a focused workbench for career decisions: calm enough for sensitive CV work, direct enough for interview preparation, and rigorous enough to make source quality visible.

## Anti-references

- Marketing landing pages that delay the main task.
- Generic AI chat surfaces that hide sources or uncertainty.
- Decorative dashboard visuals that make evidence harder to scan.
- Interfaces that imply the original CV will be modified automatically.
- Overconfident career-advice products that invent salaries, fit, culture, or candidate strengths.

## Design Principles

- Start with the task: the first screen must let the user research a company.
- Evidence before advice: report content must make sources, confidence, and uncertainty visible.
- CV safety is part of the interface: tailoring controls must clarify that the original CV is not overwritten.
- Spanish-first clarity: labels and error states should use practical Argentine Spanish.
- Responsive by default: phone, tablet, and desktop layouts must preserve form usability and report readability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion preferences, readable line lengths, wrapping for long URLs, and layouts that work on narrow mobile screens. Avoid sensitive inferences from CV content and avoid exposing raw CV text beyond the report-generation workflow.
